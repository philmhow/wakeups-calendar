# Privacy Policy — WakeUps: Oilfield Calendar

**Last updated: 11 September 2026**

WakeUps: Oilfield Calendar keeps your rotation schedule, calendar events and
reminders on your own phone.

## The short version

**This app has no account, no server, no analytics, and no internet
permission at all. Nothing you type into it ever leaves your device, because
there is no network connection it is even able to make.**

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your rotation pattern, the days you mark as worked or
off, calendar events, reminders and your settings (theme, accent colour) — is
stored in a database file inside the app's own private storage on your
device. We cannot see any of it, and none of it is transmitted anywhere.

## Permissions, and why each is asked for

**Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — to show the reminders you set.

**Alarms** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, `WAKE_LOCK`,
`VIBRATE`) — so a reminder set for a specific time arrives at that time, and
so reminders survive a phone restart.

That is the complete list. The app does not request location, camera,
contacts, storage, or internet access, and has no use for any of them.

## Android's automatic backup

Android backs up app data to **your own Google Drive**, encrypted with your
device screen lock, when the phone is idle and charging. This is a feature of
the Android operating system, not of this app, and Google cannot read the
contents. You can turn it off for this app in **Settings › Google ›
Backup** on your phone.

## The home screen widget

The optional home screen widget shows the same rotation status the app
itself does. It is drawn on your device from the same local data — nothing
about placing or viewing it sends anything anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no profile.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No third-party trackers or SDKs of any kind.
- No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, for any purpose —
  there is no mechanism in the app that could do so.

## Children

This app is a scheduling tool for working adults on rotating shifts. It is
not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently removes its storage, including your
rotation history, events and reminders. Since we hold no copy of your data,
there is nothing to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your
behalf.
