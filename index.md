# Privacy Policy — WakeUps: Oilfield Calendar

**Last updated: 2 November 2026**

WakeUps: Oilfield Calendar keeps your rotation schedule, calendar events,
reminders and hitch log on your own phone.

## The short version

**This app has no account, no server, no analytics, and no internet
permission at all. The app never sends anything you enter off your phone,
because it has no way to make a network connection of its own.**

A copy of your data can end up somewhere else only in these ways, and each is
explained below:

- You **share your dates** with someone, using your phone's share menu.
- You **export your year** as a picture, PDF or calendar file, and send or
  save it somewhere you choose.
- You **send your roster to family** with Connect, as a code they scan or a
  file you send.
- You **save a backup file** to a place you choose.
- You turn on **calendar sync**, and WakeUps writes your roster into a
  calendar on your phone. If that calendar belongs to an account such as
  Google, the account copies it, as it does any other entry.
- Android's own **automatic backup** copies app data to your Google account.
  That is a feature of your phone, not of this app, and you can turn it off.

If you buy WakeUps Pro, the purchase goes through the Google Play Store app on
your phone and is handled by Google Play, not by this app. That is explained
under "Purchases".

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored in a database file inside the app's own
private storage on your device. That is:

- your rotation pattern, including the blocks of a mixed roster, and crew
  changes you have moved earlier or later;
- the days you mark as worked or off, and any notes you write on them;
- the type you give a day: travel, standby, leave, sick or training;
- calendar events, with their times and, if set, a time zone;
- reminders;
- the hitch log, if you use it: hours worked and overtime per day, and
  whether a day was sold, standby or per diem. This is pay-related
  information that you enter yourself;
- rosters other people have shared with you through Connect (their name and
  their rotation), and the name you send your own roster under;
- your settings: theme, accent colour, whether you travel out the day before
  your first shift, and whether you sleep before travelling home;
- a few notes the app keeps for itself: which version's "What's new" card
  you have seen, and, to pick a good time to ask for a rating, how many
  times it has asked and when it last asked.

If calendar sync is on, the app also stores which calendar it writes to, that
calendar's account name and type (for a Google calendar, the account name is
usually your email address), and a list of the entries it wrote, so it can
update them later.

We cannot see any of it, and the app does not transmit any of it anywhere.

If you buy WakeUps Pro, the app also keeps a note on your phone that Pro is
unlocked, in the same private storage, so Pro keeps working with no signal.

## Permissions, and why each is asked for

**Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — to show the reminders you set.

**Alarms** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, `WAKE_LOCK`,
`VIBRATE`) — so a reminder set for a specific time arrives at that time, and
so reminders survive a phone restart.

**Google Play billing** (`com.android.vending.BILLING`) — so the app can ask
the Google Play Store app on your phone to show the WakeUps Pro purchase
screen, and ask it whether you have already bought Pro. This does not give the
app internet access. The app only talks to the Play Store app on the same
phone.

**Calendar** (`READ_CALENDAR`, `WRITE_CALENDAR`) — only if you turn on
calendar sync (Pro), and only after a screen that explains it. WakeUps reads
your calendars to list them for you and to find the entries it wrote itself.
It writes only its own entries into the one calendar you pick, and changes or
deletes nothing else. The permission works only on the calendar storage on
your phone, and gives the app no internet access.

Those are all the permissions the app asks for. Calendar is the only optional
one: Android asks you about it only when you turn calendar sync on. The app
does not request location, camera, contacts, storage, or internet access, and
has no use for any of them. Scanning a Connect code uses Google Play
services' own scanner, which needs no camera permission from WakeUps.

## Purchases

The app is free. WakeUps Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the
home screen widget, reminders, extra accent colours, exporting your year, the
hitch log, calendar sync, and sending your roster with Connect.

When you buy Pro, you pay through Google Play. Google processes the payment
under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or other
payment details.

Like every developer who sells through Google Play, we receive Google's
standard order record for the sale in our Play Console: things like the order
number, the date, the country and the price paid. We use it only to handle
refunds and questions about a purchase. We do not receive your name or
payment details from the app, and the app itself sends us nothing.

To know whether you own Pro, the app asks the Play Store app on your phone.
The answer is saved in the app's private storage so Pro works offline. If you
reinstall the app or move to a new phone, **Restore purchase** on the Pro
screen asks Google Play again. No account with us is needed.

Refunds are handled through Google Play. If a purchase is refunded, Pro
features lock again. What you entered while you had Pro stays on your phone.

## Asking for a rating

Now and then, on the last day of a hitch or a day at home, the app asks
Google Play to show its rating window. Google Play decides whether to show
it, and if you rate the app, you do it in Google Play, under Google's terms
and privacy policy. The app is not told whether the window appeared, what
you rated, or what you wrote. It asks at most three times, months apart,
and never in the first two weeks after you set up your roster. You can also
rate it any time from **Settings › About**.

## Android's automatic backup

Android can back up app data to **your own Google account** when the phone is
idle and charging, and restore it if you set up a new phone. For this app that
means a copy of what is listed under "What the app stores". Google describes
these backups as encrypted with your device screen lock.

This is a feature of the Android operating system, not of this app. The app
does not send the backup, we never receive it, and we cannot see or access it.
It belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy for
your account. You can turn it off in **Settings › Google › Backup** on your
phone, and you can delete existing backups from the same place.

## Sharing your dates

The calendar has a share button that writes a short message with your next
dates, such as "home Thu 12 Nov, back to work Thu 26 Nov". Tapping it opens
your phone's own share menu, and you choose the app and the person it goes to.
That app sends the message, not this one. Nothing is shared unless you tap
the button and pick where it goes.

## Exporting your year

With Pro you can export your year from the year view. Nothing is exported
unless you tap Share or Save, and you pick the app or the place it goes. That
app handles it under its own terms; WakeUps sends nothing itself.

- **The picture and the PDF** show the year's days on and off, nights,
  travel days, and the times you came in early or stayed late. The day types
  you mark (travel, standby, leave, sick, training) are left out unless you
  turn on "Show day types" before exporting. They carry no notes and no
  events.
- **The calendar file** holds your hitches, days at home and travel days for
  the next two years, and your events, with their titles, notes, times and
  time zones.

To share a file, WakeUps writes it to its own cache folder first. The next
share replaces it, and Android may clear the cache at any time. The app that
receives it can read that one file only.

## Connect: sharing your roster with family

Connect lets family see when you are home. Sending your roster is part of
Pro; receiving one is free.

- **How it leaves your phone.** Connect shows your roster as a QR code on
  your screen, for someone next to you to scan, or puts it in a small file
  you send with an app you choose. WakeUps sends nothing itself, and there
  is no server and no account.
- **What it holds:** the name you type, your rotation's blocks (for example
  14 days on, 14 off), the date the current cycle started, whether you sleep
  before travelling home, up to three rotation changes you have saved for
  later dates, and the times you came in early or stayed late that are still
  to come.
- **What it does not hold:** events, notes, the hitch log, day types such as
  sick or leave, your record of past days, reminders, or anything that
  identifies your phone.
- **Receiving.** A roster you scan or import is stored on your phone until
  you delete it. Scanning uses Google Play services' code scanner: Google
  Play services shows the camera screen and downloads the scanner, and
  WakeUps receives only the text of the code.

## Calendar sync

If you turn on calendar sync (Pro), WakeUps writes your roster into a
calendar on your phone, using Android's calendar storage. WakeUps itself
still makes no network connection.

What happens next depends on the calendar you pick:

- **A WakeUps calendar on this phone** stays on this phone.
- **A calendar that belongs to an account,** such as your Google account, is
  synced by that account like any other entry: to your other devices, and to
  anyone you have shared that calendar with. That is a feature of the
  account, not of this app. The copy is governed by that provider's terms and
  privacy policy. We never receive it and cannot see it.

What is written: hitches, days at home and travel days for the next two
years, and your events with their titles, notes, times and time zones. You
choose which. Your day notes, hours and day types are never written. To keep
the calendar current, WakeUps updates it when you open the app or change
something, and once or twice a day on the phone itself, even when the app is
closed.

To stop, turn sync off in **Settings › Calendar sync**. You can remove
everything WakeUps wrote, or keep it. You can also take the calendar
permission away in Android's settings at any time.

## Backup files

In Settings you can save a backup file and restore from one. The file holds
your rotation, the days you marked and their notes, day types, crew changes
you moved, events, reminders, the hitch log, the rosters others shared with
you, and your theme, accent colour and travel settings. It leaves out the
name you send your own roster under, which shared roster is marked on your
calendar, calendar sync's settings and its list of entries (which only mean
something on the phone they were made on), and the app's own notes about
"What's new" and ratings. It never includes whether you bought WakeUps Pro;
to get Pro back on a new phone, use **Restore purchase**.

Saving and restoring use your phone's own file picker, so the app needs no
storage permission, and it can only read or write the one file you pick. The
file goes wherever you choose. If you choose a cloud folder such as Google
Drive, that app uploads it, under its own terms; this app still makes no
network connection. We never receive your backup files.

## The home screen widget

The optional home screen widget (part of WakeUps Pro) shows the same rotation
status the app itself does. It is drawn on your device from the same local
data — nothing about placing or viewing it sends anything anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no profile.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No third-party trackers or analytics SDKs. The only libraries that deal
  with anything outside the app are Google's own: Play Billing, which only
  talks to the Play Store app on your phone, and only for purchases; Play's
  in-app review library, which only asks the Play Store app to show its
  rating window; and the Play services code scanner, which only reads a code
  you point the camera at.
- The app itself sells or transfers no data to anyone, for any purpose, and
  sends nothing to us. The only things that leave it are what you choose to
  send or save: a message with your dates, an exported file, a Connect code
  or file, a backup file, or the entries calendar sync writes into a
  calendar you pick.

## Children

This app is a scheduling tool for working adults on rotating shifts. It is
not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its storage from your phone, including your
rotation history, events, reminders, hitch log and the rosters others shared
with you. If Android backup was on, a copy may remain in your Google account
until you delete it there (see above). Backup and exported files you saved
stay where you saved them until you delete them. Entries calendar sync wrote
stay in that calendar until you remove them: turn sync off first and choose
to remove them, or delete them in your calendar app. Since we hold no copy of
your data ourselves, there is nothing to request from us and nothing for us
to delete on your behalf.

If you bought WakeUps Pro, Google Play keeps the purchase with your Google
account so it can be restored, and the order record described under
"Purchases" stays in Google's records and our Play Console as Google requires.
Uninstalling the app does not cancel or refund the purchase.
